Dashboard
Ride Requests

Go online from the driver dashboard to start receiving requests. Use Accept or Decline to manage the queue.

Active Ride

After accepting a ride, use Start Ride when the passenger is onboard and End Ride when the trip is finished.

Earnings

Your completed trips are added to today, weekly, and total earnings on the driver earnings page.

SOS

Use SOS only in emergencies. It sends an immediate alert with confirmation feedback.
